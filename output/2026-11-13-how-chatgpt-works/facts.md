# How ChatGPT works (Dark World, up to 1 min)
- ChatGPT made by OpenAI, released November 30, 2022 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ChatGPT — high
- ~900 million weekly active users in February 2026 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ChatGPT — high
- Built on GPT (generative pre-trained transformer) large language models — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ChatGPT — high
- Base model trained on huge amounts of text (incl. Wikipedia, code) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ChatGPT — high
- Pre-trained to predict the next word / token; text is split into tokens (words or word pieces) turned into numbers — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Large_language_model — high
- Generates the answer one token at a time — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Large_language_model — high
- Then fine-tuned with RLHF: human trainers ranked answers, rankings built a reward model to tune it — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ChatGPT — high
- Output can be inaccurate/less reliable (no lookup of stored answers) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Large_language_model — medium

## Angle
- Core: ChatGPT is a giant version of phone keyboard next-word suggestion; it writes each answer fresh, word by word.
- Misconception: it is not a brain that "knows" and looks up answers — hence it can be wrong.
