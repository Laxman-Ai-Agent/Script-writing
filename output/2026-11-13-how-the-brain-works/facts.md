# How the brain works (Health Decode, up to 1 min)
- Adult brain weighs about 3 lb (~1.4 kg) — https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/body/22638-brain — high
- ~86 billion neurons, plus about as many glial cells — Cleveland Clinic; PMC table https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3950304/table/T1 — high
- Brain ~2% of body weight but uses ~20% of body energy — https://brain.health/about-your-brain ; https://www.science.org.au/curious/node/278 ; PMC table — high
- Energy mainly used for sending/receiving signals across brain circuits — science.org.au — medium-high
- Neurons send and receive electrical and chemical signals — Cleveland Clinic — high
- Cerebrum: largest part; senses, speech, memory, movement, reasoning — Cleveland Clinic — high
- Cerebellum: back of brain; balance, posture, coordination — Cleveland Clinic — high
- Brainstem: connects to spinal cord; automatic functions (heart rate, breathing, sleep-wake, swallowing) — Cleveland Clinic — high

## Angle
- 2% of body weight, ~20% of energy — because 86 billion neurons never stop signalling
- Three parts, three jobs; brainstem keeps heart and breathing going even in sleep
