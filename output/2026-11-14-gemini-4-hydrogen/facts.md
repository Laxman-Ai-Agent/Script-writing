## Angle
- The twist: Google is not selling its smartest model but a "good enough, fast, cheap" workhorse. It jumps from 18% to 57% on Terminal-Bench and costs less than its bigger siblings, yet ranks only #5 on Agent Arena. The future of AI agents may be cheap models doing most of the work, with a big model called in only for hard parts (Orchestrator idea).
- Misconception to correct: "new model = best model". Hydrogen is explicitly not the top model; also all the benchmark numbers are Google's own, so treat "5x fewer hallucinations"-type claims with caution (that exact figure is unconfirmed).
