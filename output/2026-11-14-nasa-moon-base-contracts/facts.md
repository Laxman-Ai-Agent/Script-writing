## Angle
- The "$10 billion" is a ceiling for a 10-year bidding pool, not cash handed out; real awards so far are small: about $1.3B VADR and $94M for the rover. Twist: the nuclear lunar rover is built from Mars rover spare parts.
- Misconception: $10B was not awarded to one company; "moon base by 2032" and "$30B" are shaky (NASA says $20B for first 7 years, phase 3 to 2036+).
