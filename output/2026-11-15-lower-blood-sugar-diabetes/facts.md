# How to lower / control blood sugar in type 2 diabetes (India)
- ICMR-INDIAB 2023: ~101 million Indians have diabetes (prevalence 11.4%) — https://www.thelancet.com/journals/landia/article/PIIS2213-8587(23)00119-5/fulltext — high
- Same study: ~136 million prediabetes (15.3%); also ~315 M hypertension, ~254 M general obesity, ~351 M abdominal obesity — https://www.thelancet.com/journals/landia/article/PIIS2213-8587(23)00119-5/fulltext — high
- Food breaks down into glucose; pancreas beta cells release insulin, which acts like a key letting glucose into muscle, liver and fat cells — https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/diabetes/overview/what-is-diabetes — high
- Type 2: cells resist insulin (fat in liver/muscle, belly fat), pancreas makes more insulin, beta cells wear out over years, sugar stays high — https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/diabetes/overview/what-is-diabetes ; https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/type-2-diabetes/symptoms-causes/syc-20351193 — high
- Refined carbs (white rice, maida, sugary drinks) digest fast -> sharp glucose spike; fibre, protein, fat slow absorption and flatten the spike — https://www.cdc.gov/diabetes/healthy-eating/index.html ; https://www.hsph.harvard.edu/nutrition-source/carbohydrates/carbohydrates-and-blood-sugar/ — high
- Plate method: half non-starchy vegetables, quarter protein, quarter carbs (whole grains/starch); limit sugary drinks, juice, refined flour — https://diabetes.org/food-nutrition/diabetes-and-meal-planning ; https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/diabetes/overview/diet-eating-physical-activity — high
- Indian diets are carb-heavy (refined carbs ~62% of energy in INDIAB diet study); ICMR-NIN dietary guidelines advise more dals, veg, less polished rice/refined flour — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24910140/ ; https://www.nin.res.in/dietaryguidelines/pdfjsnew/index.php — medium (exact % from 2014 INDIAB diet analysis)
- Millets/whole grains: generally lower glycemic response than polished rice, but still carbs so portion matters — https://www.nin.res.in/dietaryguidelines/pdfjsnew/index.php ; https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/diabetes/overview/diet-eating-physical-activity — medium
- Muscle contraction moves GLUT4 to the cell surface; glucose uptake works without insulin — https://diabetesjournals.org/care/article/39/11/2065/37249 ; https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/diabetes/overview/diet-eating-physical-activity — high
- Insulin sensitivity improves after a session for ~24-48 h; gaps over 2 days lose benefit — https://diabetesjournals.org/care/article/39/11/2065/37249 ; ADA Standards of Care 2025 Sec.5 — high
- Recommended: >=150 min/week moderate activity, resistance training 2-3 non-consecutive days/week, break up sitting every ~30 min — https://diabetesjournals.org/care/issue/48/Supplement_1 ; https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/physical-activity — high
- Short walks after meals (about 10-15 min) lower post-meal glucose; 10 min after each meal beat one 30 min walk (~12% lower 3 h glucose in small study); meta-analysis supports effect — https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00125-016-3991-0 ; https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40279-022-01770-7 — medium (small trials)
- DPP (prediabetes): lifestyle with ~7% weight loss + 150 min/week cut diabetes onset 58% (metformin 31%) — https://www.niddk.nih.gov/about-niddk/research-areas/diabetes/diabetes-prevention-program-dpp ; https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa012512 — high (applies to prevention, not treating established T2D)
- ADA: 5-7%+ weight loss helps glucose; >=15% gives greater benefit incl. possible remission — https://diabetesjournals.org/care/issue/48/Supplement_1 — high
- DiRECT (Lancet 2018): 46% in remission at 12 months overall (HbA1c <6.5% off meds); 86% of those who lost >=15 kg; 57% for 10-15 kg; 34% for 5-10 kg; 7% in control — https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lancet/article/PIIS0140-6736(17)33102-1/fulltext ; https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/type-2-diabetes/ — high
- DiRECT at 2 years: 36% still in remission (remission needs maintenance) — https://www.thelancet.com/journals/landia/article/PIIS2213-8587(19)30076-2/fulltext — high
- HbA1c target <7% for many non-pregnant adults (individualised); HbA1c reflects ~3-month average — https://diabetesjournals.org/care/issue/48/Supplement_1 ; https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/diabetes/overview/tests-diagnosis/a1c-test — high
- UKPDS 35: each 1% HbA1c reduction -> 37% fewer microvascular complications, 14% fewer heart attacks, 21% fewer diabetes deaths — https://www.bmj.com/content/321/7258/405 ; https://diabetesjournals.org/care/issue/48/Supplement_1 — high
- Uncontrolled sugar damages eyes (retinopathy), kidneys, nerves, heart — https://www.cdc.gov/diabetes/about/diabetes-complications.html ; https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/diabetes/symptoms-causes/syc-20371444 — high
- Poor sleep raises insulin resistance; stress hormones (cortisol, adrenaline) raise glucose — https://www.cdc.gov/diabetes/living-with/ ; https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/diabetes/in-depth/diabetes-and-stress/art-20045130 — high
- Smokers have ~30-40% higher risk of developing T2D and harder control — https://www.cdc.gov/tobacco/campaign/tips/diseases/diabetes.html ; https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/tobacco — high
- Late heavy dinners linked to higher post-meal glucose and poorer control (circadian glucose tolerance) — https://diabetesjournals.org/care/issue/48/Supplement_1 — medium (one source, observational)
- Skipping/stopping medicines raises sugar and complication risk; never change doses without doctor — https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/type-2-diabetes/treatment/ ; https://www.cdc.gov/diabetes/treatment/ — high
- Hypoglycemia (<70 mg/dL) risk with insulin/sulfonylureas, esp. when exercising more or cutting carbs; symptoms: shakes, sweating, confusion; carry fast sugar; doctor may adjust dose — https://diabetesjournals.org/care/issue/48/Supplement_1 ; https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/low-blood-sugar-hypoglycaemia/ — high
- Asian Indians develop T2D at lower BMI and younger age (more belly/liver fat) — https://www.thelancet.com/journals/landia/article/PIIS2213-8587(23)00119-5/fulltext ; https://diabetesjournals.org/care/issue/48/Supplement_1 — medium
## Angle
- Core twist: sugar control is mostly about making cells listen to insulin again; a 10-15 min walk after meals and muscle work let muscles pull glucose in WITHOUT insulin (GLUT4); ~15 kg loss gave 86% remission in DiRECT; the exercise effect lasts only 24-48 h so daily consistency matters.
- Misconception to correct: "avoid sweets only / rice is fine"; in India refined carbs (white rice, maida) spike sugar like sweets. Also "diabetes is hopeless" (remission possible but needs maintenance) and "stop medicine once sugar is normal" (dangerous).
