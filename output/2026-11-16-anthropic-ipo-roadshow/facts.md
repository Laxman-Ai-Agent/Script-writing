## Angle
- Core twist: the company's own suppliers (chip makers + cloud) are pouring $26B in, and it earned $17B in one quarter yet still lost $536M — AI is a money-hungry business where chip sellers become shareholders.
- Misconception: "IPO already happened / you can buy now." No: roadshow only started, pricing expected Nov 18, shares not yet tradeable. Also $64.35B is the raise at top price, not the company's valuation.
- Stronger twist: the range implies ~$1.4T, above the $965B private round in May but below the ~$2T once whispered; and Q3 flipped from +$974M operating profit to a loss of about half a billion because it paid premium prices for emergency compute, with ~$895B of compute commitments looming.
