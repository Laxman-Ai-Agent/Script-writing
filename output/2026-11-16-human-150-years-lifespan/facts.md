## Lifespan ceiling
- Pyrkov et al. 2021 (Nature Communications, May 2021): blood-count "DOSI" resilience falls with age; extrapolated resilience hits zero at ~120-150 years = proposed absolute limit. It is an extrapolation, not an observed ceiling; authors tied to Gero (company) — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8149842 — high (paper); medium (interpretation)
- Scientific American coverage of Pyrkov: calls 150 speculative — https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/humans-could-live-up-to-150-years-new-research-suggests/ — high
- Jeanne Calment, 122 years 164 days, died 1997 (born 1875), longest verified life — https://www.statnews.com/2024/10/07/life-expectancy-reaches-limit-new-human-longevity-study-journal-nature-aging/ (mentions record) — high
- Dong, Milholland, Vijg 2016 Nature: max human lifespan ~115 years (limit ~ 115, rare outliers to ~125) — https://www.nature.com/articles/nature19793 (bg) — medium (not re-fetched; disputed by other statisticians)
- Olshansky et al. 2024 Nature Aging: life expectancy gains are slowing; since 1990 almost no studied population gained 3 yrs/decade; survival to 100 unlikely to exceed ~15% women / ~5% men; radical extension implausible this century unless aging itself is slowed — https://www.statnews.com/2024/10/07/life-expectancy-reaches-limit-new-human-longevity-study-journal-nature-aging/ — high
- "Curing cancer adds only ~3 years" (user note): not confirmed in this research; Olshansky's earlier work says roughly that, but unverified here — https://www.statnews.com/2024/10/07/life-expectancy-reaches-limit-new-human-longevity-study-journal-nature-aging/ — disputed (unverified)

## Angle
- Core twist: the "150" number is a math extrapolation of lost body resilience (Pyrkov), not proof anyone can get there; record is still Calment's 122 (1997), and the average person worldwide lives ~71. AI is accelerating discovery (reprogramming, drugs, screening), but real human results so far are small: mouse eyes, a 71-patient lung trial, early eye-injection safety trial. Realistic win: more healthy years, not 150.
- Misconception: "AI/biotech will let us live to 150 soon." Reality: no human has reversed aging; Olshansky says gains are slowing; the best AI results are in specific diseases (cancer screening: 12% fewer interval cancers; IPF drug), which is what actually adds years today.
- Hook idea: "150 years is not a dream number — it's where a scientific formula says your body's recovery power hits zero."
- Practical close: what extends healthy life now (screening, BP/sugar control, exercise) — cite WHO/NIH, not hype.
