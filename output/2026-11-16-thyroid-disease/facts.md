# Thyroid disease (hypothyroidism, hyperthyroidism, goitre, Hashimoto's, Graves')
- Thyroid = butterfly-shaped gland at front of neck; makes T3/T4 which set metabolism, heart rate, body temperature (standard physiology; not fetched this run) — https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/endocrine-diseases — medium
- TSH loop: hypothalamus -> pituitary TSH -> thyroid; high TSH = underactive thyroid, low TSH = overactive (standard endocrinology; not fetched this run) — https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/diagnostic-tests/thyroid — medium
- Hypothyroidism: Hashimoto's (autoimmune) is the most common cause; others: thyroiditis, surgery, radiation, some drugs (heart/bipolar/cancer), congenital, rarely iodine imbalance — https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/endocrine-diseases/hypothyroidism — high
- Hypo symptoms: fatigue, weight gain, feeling cold, dry skin, hair thinning, joint/muscle pain, heavy/irregular periods, fertility problems, slow heart rate, depression; develops slowly over months/years — https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/endocrine-diseases/hypothyroidism — high
- Hypo symptoms also: constipation, poor concentration, hoarse voice — https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/underactive-thyroid-hypothyroidism/ — high
- Women much more likely than men to get hypothyroidism; also more common over 60 — https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/endocrine-diseases/hypothyroidism — high (exact ratio not verified)
- Hyper: Graves' disease (autoimmune) is the most common cause; others: overactive nodules, thyroiditis, excess iodine (seaweed, some cough syrups/medicines), too much thyroid tablet — https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/endocrine-diseases/hyperthyroidism — high
- Hyper symptoms: weight loss despite hunger, fast/irregular heartbeat, nervousness, poor sleep, tremor, sweating/heat intolerance, frequent stools, goitre; older adults may look depressed/withdrawn — https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/endocrine-diseases/hyperthyroidism — high
- Hyper treatments: beta-blockers (symptom relief in hours, don't cut hormone), antithyroid drugs (methimazole; PTU early pregnancy; rare liver/white-cell risks), radioactive iodine (nearly everyone later becomes hypothyroid; not in pregnancy/breastfeeding), surgery (large goitre; full removal = lifelong tablet) — https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/endocrine-diseases/hyperthyroidism — high
- Untreated hyper risks: irregular heartbeat -> clots, stroke, heart failure; Graves' eye disease; osteoporosis; menstrual/fertility issues — https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/endocrine-diseases/hyperthyroidism — high
- Thyroid storm = sudden severe worsening of hyper symptoms (medical emergency) — https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/endocrine-diseases/hyperthyroidism — high
- Myxedema coma = rare, life-threatening complication of severe untreated hypothyroidism; needs immediate treatment — https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/endocrine-diseases/hypothyroidism — high
- Levothyroxine = same hormone a healthy thyroid makes; blood test 6-8 weeks after starting, then every 6 months, then yearly; usually lifelong (NHS); never stop without doctor; overdose -> atrial fibrillation/osteoporosis — https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/endocrine-diseases/hypothyroidism — high
- Take levothyroxine on empty stomach, gap of ~4 hours from calcium/iron (standard clinical guidance; Mayo page blocked, not fetched) — https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/hypothyroidism/diagnosis-treatment/drc-20350290 — medium
- Pregnancy: untreated hypothyroidism affects mother and baby; medicine is safe; many women need a higher dose, tell doctor immediately when pregnant — https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/endocrine-diseases/hypothyroidism — high
- Congenital hypothyroidism exists (present at birth); newborn screening is standard practice (screening detail not verified from fetched source) — https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/endocrine-diseases/hypothyroidism — medium
- Post-pregnancy (postpartum) thyroiditis is a recognised thyroiditis type (not fetched) — https://www.thyroid.org/postpartum-thyroiditis/ — medium
- India: ~42 million people with thyroid disease, quoted by Indian Thyroid Society (2008) and repeated by a minister at ITS conference Feb 2026; it is a projection/estimate, not a national survey count — https://indiaenvironmentportal.org.in/news-clippings/42-m-indians-suffer-from-thyroid-disease — medium
- India hypothyroidism ~11% (vs UK 2%, USA 4.6%) per Down To Earth; study source unclear; other studies lower: Kerala 3.9%, north India 9.3% — https://www.downtoearth.org.in/news/health/1-in-10-indians-have-hypothyroidism-61693 — disputed (varies 4-11% by study)
- India salt iodisation: National Goitre Control Programme 1962 (after 1956 Kangra experiment); universal salt iodisation adopted 1992 — https://thesouthfirst.com/health/goitre-down-thyroid-autoimmunity-up-what-four-decades-of-iodised-salt-show-in-indian-children/ — medium
- Household iodised salt coverage: <5% (1985) -> 51% (2005-06) -> 93% (NFHS-4, 2015-16); 2014-15 national survey: 91.7% any iodised salt, 77.5% adequately iodised — https://www.cambridge.org/core/product/253432E5316EE09DB001D2A1974A1E47/core-reader — medium
- School-child goitre fell from 23.5% (2003) to 10.3% in a study of 8,903 children; mild residual goitre persists; gaps: crystal/unpackaged salt, washing salt before use, rural coverage lower — https://thesouthfirst.com/health/goitre-down-thyroid-autoimmunity-up-what-four-decades-of-iodised-salt-show-in-indian-children/ — medium
- NHS: low iodine intake is rare in UK; iodine deficiency is the classic worldwide cause of goitre (WHO fact sheet URL 404; not verified) — https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/underactive-thyroid-hypothyroidism/ — medium
- Goitrogens myth: cooked cabbage/cauliflower/soy in normal amounts are fine; issue only with huge raw amounts or soy near tablet time (not verified from fetched source; Mayo blocked) — https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/hypothyroidism/in-depth/hypothyroidism-diet/art-20048392 — medium
## Angle
- Core insight: thyroid is a tiny neck gland that sets the speed of every organ; symptoms (tiredness, weight, mood) are so ordinary that people ignore them for years; ~1 in 10 Indians may have hypothyroidism, mostly women, and a cheap daily tablet controls it if taken right and never stopped on own.
- Twist: India largely beat iodine-deficiency goitre with salt, but autoimmune (Hashimoto's) is now the commonest cause; too much iodine or too much tablet can also harm.
- Misconceptions: "thyroid is only women's disease" (men get it too, less often); "stop tablet when feel better" (no, usually lifelong); "all weight gain is thyroid" (it causes only modest weight gain); "cabbage/soy banned" (normal cooked amounts fine).
