## Why it forms
- Began as upper-air cyclonic circulation over south Andaman Sea -> low-pressure -> well-marked LPA over SE Bay -> depression — https://www.theweek.in/news/india/2026/11/13/will-it-rain-in-chennai-cyclone-akwan-may-bring-showers-from-november-18.amp.html (13 Nov), Utkarsh — high
- Warm sea waters + favourable atmosphere fuel it; unusually active post-monsoon season — Utkarsh (16 Nov) — medium
- General science (common knowledge, no fetched source): sea surface ~26.5C+ gives heat/moisture, rising warm air creates low pressure, Coriolis effect spins it — medium
