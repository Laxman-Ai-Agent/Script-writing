## Angle
- Third storm in ~3 weeks hitting the same Andhra coast (Arnab Oct 28, Murasu Nov 3, now Akwan ~Nov 19-20): same warm Bay of Bengal waters feeding storms back-to-back; AP people have no time to recover.
- Name from Iran (Telugu viewers don't expect an Iranian name for an Indian-ocean storm: 13 countries rotate names). Meaning "evil mind" only single-source — say "reportedly" or skip.
- Misconception: name does not mean it's stronger/worse; naming is just for easy tracking. Also, forecast is not certain — track/landfall can shift; rely on IMD, not viral posts.
