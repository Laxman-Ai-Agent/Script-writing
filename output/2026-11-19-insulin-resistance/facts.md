# Insulin resistance
- Insulin is a hormone made by the pancreas; it moves glucose from blood into muscle, fat and liver cells to be used for energy or stored — https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/22206-insulin-resistance — high
- Insulin resistance = muscle, fat and liver cells respond poorly to insulin — https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/diabetes/overview/what-is-diabetes/prediabetes-insulin-resistance — high
- Glucose builds up in blood, so pancreas makes more insulin (hyperinsulinemia) to compensate; when it cannot keep up, blood sugar rises -> prediabetes / type 2 diabetes — https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/22206-insulin-resistance — high
- Insulin resistance can raise blood glucose and contribute to weight gain — https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/diabetes/overview/what-is-diabetes/prediabetes-insulin-resistance — high
- Excess body fat, especially visceral (belly/organ) fat, is considered a primary driver — https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/22206-insulin-resistance — high
- Exact cause not fully understood by researchers; risk factors known — https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/diabetes/overview/what-is-diabetes/prediabetes-insulin-resistance — high
- Risk factors: overweight/large waist, inactivity, family history of diabetes, age 35+ (NIDDK) / 45+ (Cleveland), gestational diabetes history, PCOS, sleep apnea, smoking, some long-term medicines (general only) — both NIDDK and Cleveland URLs above — high
- Cleveland also lists diets high in processed food/refined carbs, and hormonal conditions (Cushing's, hypothyroidism) — https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/22206-insulin-resistance — medium
- Asian origin listed as a higher-risk group — https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/diabetes/overview/what-is-diabetes/prediabetes-insulin-resistance — high
- Usually NO symptoms for years while pancreas keeps up — https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/22206-insulin-resistance — high
- Possible signs: darkened skin in body folds (acanthosis nigricans), skin tags — https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/22206-insulin-resistance — medium (not on NIDDK page; single source)
- High-blood-sugar symptoms: increased thirst and urination, hunger, fatigue, blurred vision, unexplained weight loss — https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/22206-insulin-resistance — high
- Insulin resistance itself is not routinely tested (mainly research); prediabetes is found by blood tests — https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/diabetes/overview/what-is-diabetes/prediabetes-insulin-resistance — high
- Prediabetes ranges: A1C 5.7–6.4%, fasting glucose 100–125 mg/dL — https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/diabetes/overview/what-is-diabetes/prediabetes-insulin-resistance — high
- Providers may order fasting glucose, A1c, lipid panel; doctor decides — https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/22206-insulin-resistance — high
- Prevention: healthy eating, regular activity, weight management, enough sleep — https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/diabetes/overview/what-is-diabetes/prediabetes-insulin-resistance — high
- Diabetes Prevention Program: losing 5–7% of starting body weight lowered diabetes risk — https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/diabetes/overview/what-is-diabetes/prediabetes-insulin-resistance — high
- Prediabetes gives a high chance of type 2 diabetes within 5–10 years, but not everyone — https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/diabetes/overview/what-is-diabetes/prediabetes-insulin-resistance — high
- US prediabetes: 97.6 million adults in 2021 (NIDDK) vs 115.2 million (CDC page); differs by year/source, so say "around 100 million, ధృవీకరించుకోవాలి exact year" — https://www.cdc.gov/diabetes/php/data-research/index.html — disputed
- US diabetes: 40.1 million (12.0%) in 2023; 27.6% of those undiagnosed — https://www.cdc.gov/diabetes/php/data-research/index.html — high
- India (ICMR-INDIAB, Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology, June 2023): diabetes 11.4% of adults, prediabetes 15.3% — https://www.thelancet.com/journals/landia/article/PIIS2213-8587(23)00119-5/fulltext (seen via news summaries; Lancet URL not opened, ధృవీకరించుకోవాలి) — medium
- India: ~136 million with prediabetes (press figure) — https://www.downtoearth.org.in/health/india-s-diabetes-epidemic-just-got-more-grim-lancet-report-reveals-why-89946 — disputed (secondary source only, ధృవీకరించుకోవాలి)
## Angle
- Twist: the body is not "failing to burn food"; cells stop listening, so the pancreas shouts louder (more insulin), and high insulin itself favours fat storage, so the cycle feeds itself
- Often silent for years; the pancreas compensates, so blood sugar can look normal while insulin is already high
- Misconception: "insulin resistance = only sugar eaters / only diabetics / only overweight people"
## Myths vs facts
- ❌ Insulin resistance means diabetes already — ✅ It usually comes first; prediabetes can often be delayed with lifestyle
- ❌ Only overweight people get it — ✅ Weight is a major risk factor, but genetics, age, PCOS, sleep, inactivity also matter
- ❌ You would feel symptoms — ✅ Usually no symptoms for years
- ❌ You need extreme weight loss — ✅ 5–7% weight loss showed benefit in the Diabetes Prevention Program
- ❌ Sugar alone is the cause — ✅ Causes are many (excess belly fat, inactivity, sleep, genetics); no single food is the cause
- ❌ Dark neck patches are just dirt — ✅ They can be acanthosis nigricans, a possible sign; ask a doctor
