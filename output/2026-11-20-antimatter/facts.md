# Antimatter – what happens when it meets matter
- Antimatter particles have the same mass as their matter twins but opposite charge (positron = anti-electron, antiproton, antihydrogen = antiproton + positron) — https://home.cern/science/physics/antimatter — high
- Dirac's equation (1928) predicted antimatter; Nobel Prize 1933 — https://home.cern/science/physics/antimatter — high
- Carl Anderson discovered the positron in 1932 in cosmic-ray cloud-chamber photos — https://home.cern/science/physics/antimatter — high
- Antiproton discovered 1955 at Berkeley Bevatron (Segre, Chamberlain) — background knowledge, not re-fetched; verify at britannica.com/science/antiproton — medium
- First antihydrogen atoms made at CERN in 1995 (few, fast, not trapped); trapped/cold antihydrogen came later (ALPHA 2010-11) — background knowledge, not re-fetched — medium
- Annihilation converts nearly all mass to energy, ~50x more efficient than fusion — https://home.cern/science/physics/antimatter — high
- 1 g antimatter + 1 g matter = 2 g x c^2 = 1.8e14 J ≈ 43 kt TNT (own E=mc^2 calculation); Hiroshima ≈ 15 kt (widely cited) — high
- Electron-positron annihilation gives two 511 keV gamma photons (basis of PET) — standard physics; not re-fetched — high
- CERN Antimatter Factory (AD + ELENA) delivers ~400 million antiprotons/hour; a full year of antihydrogen ≈ 30 million atoms; a year of antiprotons would release only ~500 J (a 100 W bulb for ~5 s) — https://home.cern/science/physics/antimatter — high
- Cost ~$62.5 trillion per gram is a NASA-era estimate widely quoted; original source NOT verified this session — disputed (say "often quoted estimate, trillions of dollars per gram")
- ALPHA trapped antihydrogen for 1000 s in 2011 (Nature Physics) — background knowledge, not re-fetched — medium
- Today ALPHA stores antihydrogen up to ~100 hours, makes up to ~3,000 atoms/hour, measured properties to 12 digits — https://home.cern/science/physics/antimatter — high
- ALPHA-g (Nature 621, 27 Sept 2023): antihydrogen falls down like matter; precision within ~25% of normal gravity, not exact — https://news.berkeley.edu/2023/09/27/antimatter-embraces-earth-falling-downward-like-normal-matter/ and https://www.yorku.ca/news/?p=18238 — high
- BASE-STEP: 24 Mar 2026 (reported 25 Mar) a portable Penning trap with 92 antiprotons was driven ~30 min around CERN site, up to 42 km/h; 2024 rehearsal with ~105 protons for 4 hours — https://physicsworld.com/a/researchers-at-cern-transport-antiprotons-by-truck-in-world-first-experiment/ and https://www.riken.jp/en/news_pubs/news/2026/20260325_2/index.html — high
- CERN antimatter page says the lorry transport was "November 2025", conflicting with March 2026 in Physics World/RIKEN; use March 2026 (2 sources) or say "recently" — https://home.cern/science/physics/antimatter — disputed
- Penning trap: magnetic + electric fields hold charged antiparticles in vacuum so they never touch matter; neutral atoms held by magnetic trap — standard; not re-fetched — high
- Bananas emit a positron roughly every ~75 minutes (potassium-40 decay); thunderstorms/lightning also make positrons (NASA Fermi gamma-ray flashes) — background knowledge, not re-fetched — medium
- PET scans use positron emitters (e.g. fluorine-18) in hospitals daily — standard medical fact — high
- Big Bang should have made equal matter and antimatter; leading idea is ~1 extra matter particle per billion pairs; unsolved — https://home.cern/science/physics/antimatter — high
- BASE-type traps hold 100-1000 antiparticles; full annihilation ≈ a millionth of a joule — https://home.cern/science/physics/antimatter — high
- Future: PET (existing), antiproton cancer-therapy research (CERN ACE), antimatter propulsion only conceptual — background knowledge — medium

## Angle
- Twist: antimatter is the most energy-dense thing known, yet everything humans ever made would barely light a bulb for seconds; the deeper mystery is why we exist at all (matter won by ~1 in a billion). You also meet it daily (banana, PET scan).
- Misconception: "antimatter bomb" (Angels & Demons) — impractical; quantities are nanograms, cost astronomical, bulk storage impossible. Also: antimatter is not "anti-gravity" (ALPHA-g shows it falls down).
