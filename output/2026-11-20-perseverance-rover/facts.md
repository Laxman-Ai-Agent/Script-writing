# NASA Perseverance rover
- Launched July 30, 2020 (Atlas V, Cape Canaveral); landed Feb 18, 2021 in Jezero Crater — https://science.nasa.gov/mission/mars-2020-perseverance/ — high
- Jezero = ancient lake with river delta; crater ~45 km wide (not re-verified this session, from NASA general knowledge) — https://science.nasa.gov/mission/mars-2020-perseverance/ — medium
- Goals: look for signs of ancient microbial life, study geology/climate, cache samples for return to Earth, MOXIE oxygen demo, Ingenuity helicopter — https://science.nasa.gov/mission/mars-2020-perseverance/ — high
- Ingenuity: 72 flights, ended Jan 2024 (not re-verified this session) — https://science.nasa.gov/mission/mars-2020-perseverance/ — medium
- Odometer 28.10 mi (45.22 km) on Oct 11, 2026 (sol 2,006), passing Opportunity's record 28.06 mi (45.16 km) = off-world driving record — https://science.nasa.gov/blogs/perseverance-mars-rover/2026/10/12/nasas-perseverance-sets-off-world-record-for-total-distance-driven/ ; also https://earthsky.org/space/nasas-perseverance-rover-on-mars-sets-off-world-driving-record/ and https://petapixel.com/2026/10/13/nasa-perseverance-mars-rover-sets-new-record-for-off-world-distance-driven/ — high
- Over 90% of distance driven with AutoNav (self-driving); Opportunity/Spirit about 5% — same NASA blog Oct 12, 2026 — high
- Passed marathon distance (26.2 mi) on June 14, 2026; single-sol record 411.7 m on June 19, 2025 — https://www.tpr.org/2026-10-13/nasas-mars-rover-perseverance-has-broken-a-landmark-distance-record (single source) — medium
- 29 rock and regolith samples collected as of Oct 2026 (NASA blog). Mid-2026 reports said 27; NASA mission page says only "more than two dozen" — https://science.nasa.gov/blogs/perseverance-mars-rover/2026/10/12/nasas-perseverance-sets-off-world-record-for-total-distance-driven/ — medium (count differs by source/date)
- Rover carried 43 tubes in total; 10 tubes dropped at Three Forks depot (Dec 2022 - Jan 28, 2023; last was a witness tube) — https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/nasas-perseverance-rover-completes-mars-sample-depot/ — high
- Cheyava Falls rock (July 2024) with "leopard spots"; Sapphire Canyon sample cored from it; Nature paper Sept 10, 2025 (Hurowitz et al., "Redox-driven mineral and organic associations in Jezero Crater, Mars", Nature 645, 332-340, DOI 10.1038/s41586-025-09413-0) — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12422973 ; NASA release https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/nasa-says-mars-rover-discovered-potential-biosignature-last-year/ — high
- Spots carry vivianite (iron phosphate) and greigite (iron sulfide); organic carbon seems to have driven the redox reactions at low temperature; rock is in Bright Angel formation (mudstone) — same Nature/PMC link — high
- NASA wording: "potential biosignature" = may have biological origin but needs more data/study to tell if life is present or absent; abiotic explanations "less likely" but "we cannot rule them out" (Katie Stack Morgan); acting admin Sean Duffy: "closest we have ever come to discovering life on Mars" — NASA release Sep 2025 link above — high
- Same minerals can form without life (high heat, acid), but rock shows no sign of these; unknown if organics could have catalysed it at low temp — NASA release link above; Imperial College https://www.imperial.ac.uk/news/268353/potential-biosignatures-found-ancient-mars-lake/ — high
- Rim campaign: reached crater rim Dec 2024 area; Krokodillen (clay-bearing, older than Jezero impact) then Lac de Charmes west of rim in 2026 — https://science.nasa.gov/blog/a-journey-to-the-depths-of-ancient-mars/ ; EGU26 abstract https://meetingorganizer.copernicus.org/EGU26/EGU26-9472.html — medium (Dec 2024 rim date from memory, not re-verified)
- Lac de Charmes (about 6 months): field of light-toned rocks, many look like gabbro (deep magma rock); "Idubi" possible breccia outcrop; idea that blocks were excavated from depth by ancient impacts — NASA blog Sept 29, 2026 (Alex Jones, Imperial) link above — medium (interpretation, not confirmed)
- Now (Oct 2026) driving toward Idubi in Lac de Charmes area off west rim, hoping to find traces of ancient hydrothermal systems; next heads south toward "Rainbow Falls", a ~328 ft (100 m) block of ancient crust uplifted by the Jezero impact — NASA blog Oct 12, 2026 link above (single source for Rainbow Falls) — medium

## Angle
- Core twist: a car-sized robot became the most-travelled vehicle ever off Earth (45 km, mostly self-driven) and found the best hint of ancient life yet, but the plan to bring its 29 samples home was cancelled, so the answer is sitting in tubes on Mars.
- Misconception: "potential biosignature" does not mean life found. NASA says it needs more data; abiotic cause "less likely" but not ruled out. Also: Ingenuity ended in 2024, but the rover itself is still working.
