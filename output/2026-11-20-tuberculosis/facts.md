# Tuberculosis (TB)
- TB is caused by bacteria (Mycobacterium tuberculosis) and usually affects the lungs — https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/tuberculosis-tb/ — high
- Spreads through air when a person with active lung/throat TB coughs, speaks or sings; germs can linger for hours, more in poorly ventilated indoor spaces — https://www.cdc.gov/tb/about/index.html — high
- NHS: catching TB usually needs repeated/prolonged close contact breathing in droplets (not casual contact) — https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/tuberculosis-tb/ — high
- Touching / sharing utensils: CDC and NHS pages describe only airborne spread; neither lists touching or utensils as a route. "Not spread by touching/utensils" is consistent but not explicitly stated on these pages — https://www.cdc.gov/tb/about/index.html — medium (ధృవీకరించుకోవాలి: quote exact line from CDC "How TB spreads" or WHO Q&A before saying it strongly)
- Risk factors: diabetes, weak immunity (HIV), undernutrition, tobacco use, harmful alcohol use — https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/tuberculosis — high
- Other risk factors: close contact with an active TB patient, living in crowded group settings (prisons, shelters), children under 5, heavy drinking/smoking — https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/tuberculosis-tb/ and https://www.cdc.gov/tb/about/index.html — high
- WHY risk rises (explanatory, general physiology, not a numeric claim): weak immunity (HIV, diabetes, undernutrition) = macrophages/immune cells cannot keep the germ walled in; crowded/poorly ventilated = more germs inhaled for longer; smoking/alcohol damage lungs/immunity — consistent with WHO/CDC risk lists — medium
- Latent TB: germs in body, no symptoms, NOT contagious; can become active later — https://www.cdc.gov/tb/about/index.html and https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/tuberculosis-tb/ — high
- About a quarter of the world's population is estimated to carry TB bacteria without being sick (latent) — https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/tuberculosis — high (single body; CDC page fetched did not restate the number)
- Biological steps (inhaled droplets -> alveoli -> macrophages engulf -> bacteria survive inside -> granuloma walls off -> latent -> reactivation when immunity weakens -> tissue damage/cavities): standard textbook pathogenesis; NOT confirmed on the pages fetched — medium (ధృవీకరించుకోవాలి: cite CDC/NIH pathogenesis page, e.g. CDC "Clinical overview/Pathogenesis" or NIH NIAID TB page)
- Extrapulmonary TB: can spread to glands, bones, skin, abdomen, brain/spinal cord (meningitis), around the heart — https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/tuberculosis-tb/ — high
- Symptoms: prolonged cough (sometimes with blood), chest pain, weakness/fatigue, weight loss, fever, night sweats — https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/tuberculosis — high
- CDC and NHS use cough of 3 weeks or longer; appetite loss, chills also listed — https://www.cdc.gov/tb/about/index.html — high
- India NTEP uses cough of 2 weeks (screening criterion) — not verified on a fetched page (tbcindia/PIB pages blocked or search-only) — medium (ధృవీకరించుకోవాలి: confirm at https://tbcindia.mohfw.gov.in)
- Global 2025 data (WHO fact sheet, current version): about 10.6 million fell ill with TB, about 1.13 million died (incl. ~137,000 with HIV) — https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/tuberculosis — high (year 2025)
- Previous WHO Global TB Report 2025 (data year 2024): 10.7 million ill, 1.23 million deaths; India about 25% of global cases — https://www.who.int/teams/global-programme-on-tuberculosis-and-lung-health/tb-reports — high (reported via press: https://scroll.in/latest/1088510/india-recorded-highest-tuberculosis-cases-globally-in-2024-who ; https://thesouthfirst.com/health/india-accounts-for-one-in-four-global-tb-cases-reports-38-fall-in-deaths-who/). Use ONE year consistently in script.
- India share: about 1 in 4 global TB cases (2024 data, 2 independent news reports of WHO report; South First also reports about 25% of cases in revised 2025 data) — https://thesouthfirst.com/health/india-accounts-for-one-in-four-global-tb-cases-reports-38-fall-in-deaths-who/ — medium (WHO fact-sheet India wording was garbled; ధృవీకరించుకోవాలి against WHO report/country profile)
- India incidence fell 21%, from 237 to 187 per lakh (2015 to 2024) — WHO Global TB Report 2025 as cited by https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2203004 (page not directly fetched) and https://m.thewire.in/article/health/india-records-21-drop-in-tb-incidence-who-report — medium
- India notified record 26.07 lakh TB cases in 2024 (25.5 lakh in 2023) — Ministry of Health via press — medium (ధృవీకరించుకోవాలి)
- Standard treatment for drug-sensitive TB: daily antibiotics for 4-6 months; stopping early can cause drug resistance — https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/tuberculosis — high
- NHS: active TB usually at least 6 months of antibiotics; latent TB 3-6 months; finish the full course even if feeling better — https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/tuberculosis-tb/ — high
- Drug-resistant TB: only about 2 in 5 people with drug-resistant TB accessed treatment in 2025; shorter 6-month regimens used for 42% of rifampicin-resistant patients (do not name drugs in script) — https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/tuberculosis — high
- BCG: given to babies/young children in some countries; helps prevent severe TB forms and TB deaths in children; limited protection against adult lung TB (limits statement: ధృవీకరించుకోవాలి with WHO/CDC BCG page) — https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/tuberculosis — high for first part, medium for limits
- Prevention: cough hygiene, seek care early for symptoms, screening for high-risk people, TB preventive treatment (TPT) if prescribed, ventilation/respirators in health settings — https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/tuberculosis — high
- India Ni-kshay Poshan Yojana: monthly nutrition support for TB patients raised from Rs 500 to Rs 1,000 from 1 Nov 2024, by DBT — https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2Fmonthly-nutrition-support-for-tb-patients-doubled-to-rs-1000-j-p-nadda-3222905 and https://theprint.in/india/monthly-nutrition-support-for-tb-patients-doubled-to-rs-1000-nadda/2301422 — medium (news reports of Ministry announcement; confirm on https://tbcindia.mohfw.gov.in)
- Free TB diagnosis and treatment at government centres under NTEP — https://tbcindia.mohfw.gov.in — medium (not fetched; ధృవీకరించుకోవాలి)
- Complications if untreated/stopped: drug resistance, TB returning, spread to others and to other organs (brain, bones, heart lining) — https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/tuberculosis ; https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/tuberculosis-tb/ — high (lung damage/cavities: general, medium)
- Tests (sputum test, chest X-ray, molecular tests): general; no fetched page quoted — medium (ధృవీకరించుకోవాలి)

## Angle
- Core insight: about a quarter of the world carries the TB germ, yet most never fall sick — the immune system walls it up. TB becomes dangerous only when immunity weakens or treatment is left incomplete. And TB is curable, free in India.
- Misconception to correct: TB spreads by touching/sharing plates, is hereditary, or only affects the poor; people stop medicine once they feel better.

## Myths vs facts
- ❌ TB is hereditary — ✅ it is a bacterial infection spread through air, not through genes
- ❌ TB spreads by sharing plates / touching — ✅ spreads through air when a person with active lung TB coughs/speaks (ధృవీకరించుకోవాలి: exact quote)
- ❌ TB cannot be cured — ✅ curable with full course of treatment (usually 6 months for drug-sensitive)
- ❌ Only poor people get TB — ✅ anyone can; malnutrition, diabetes, HIV, smoking, crowding raise risk
- ❌ Stop medicine when you feel better — ✅ stopping early can bring TB back and cause drug resistance
- ❌ Every TB is contagious — ✅ latent TB is not contagious; usually only active lung/throat TB spreads

## Hook ideas
1. ప్రపంచంలో ప్రతి నలుగురిలో ఒకరి శరీరంలో TB germ ఉంటుంది — కానీ చాలామందికి ఎప్పటికీ జబ్బు రాదు. ఎందుకు?
2. Cough రెండు వారాలు దాటిందా? దాన్ని "మామూలు దగ్గు" అనుకోవద్దు.
3. TB అంటే పాత కాలం జబ్బు అనుకుంటున్నారా? ఇప్పటికీ ప్రపంచంలో ప్రతి ఏటా కోట్ల మంది దీని బారిన పడుతున్నారు.
4. TB పక్కన కూర్చుంటే వస్తుందా? plate share చేస్తే వస్తుందా? నిజం ఏంటో చూద్దాం.
5. TB పూర్తిగా నయమవుతుంది — కానీ ఒక్క తప్పు చేస్తే అది మళ్ళీ బలంగా తిరిగి వస్తుంది.
