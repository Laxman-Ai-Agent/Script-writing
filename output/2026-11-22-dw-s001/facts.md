# Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2: two names, same AI

## What it is
- Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 are described as one model; the only difference is safeguards (locks that block help with dangerous tasks).
- Mythos has looser locks on cyber security and biology; given only to companies verified via Cyber Verification Program and Life Sciences program.
- Fable is the broad-access version: Pro, Max, Team, Enterprise users and developers; also on Amazon, Google Cloud, Microsoft platforms.

## How it works / how it happened
- Fermat's Last Theorem proof made computer-checkable: took Anthropic 11 days earlier; Fable 5.2 did it in 30 hours.
- Mythos first appeared in April this year; restricted to some organizations because of its hacking skills.
- About two months later, Fable released with more safeguards for everyone.
- September: 5.1 version released.
- November 9: Anthropic released Mythos 5.2 alongside Fable 5.2.

## Why
- Two names separate broad access (Fable) from restricted access (Mythos) for the same model.
- Anthropic says Opus 5.5 is for everyday work; Fable and Mythos are for hard, large technical tasks.
