# Jensen Huang resigns as NVIDIA CEO; Colette Kress interim CEO

## What it is
- Jensen Huang resigned with immediate effect from NVIDIA CEO, President and board member posts.
- He co-founded NVIDIA in 1993 and led it for 33 years.
- Colette Kress (CFO since 2013) is interim CEO and keeps CFO duties.

## How it works / how it happened
- Nov 8-9: NVIDIA board began an investigation over allegations that Chinese intelligence targeted Huang.
- Reports said Huang admitted an affair; board said there was no reason to think company secrets or national security were harmed.
- US President Trump called Huang an honorable man and said he should continue.
- NVIDIA share price fell that day.
- Nov 11: board accepted the resignation.
- Nov 12: company announced it.
- Harvey C. Jones (on the board since 1993) became NVIDIA's first Board Chair.
- Jones said the board has full confidence in Kress and the leadership team.

## Why
- Company release and US government filing gave no reason.
- CNBC report: resignation followed reports of two affairs, one with a company employee and one with a woman US intelligence believes is a Chinese spy.
- Timing: results due five days later; Huang is absent from the first results.

## Twist / surprising facts
- August: NVIDIA agreed deals with Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs, KKR to raise over $500 billion for data centers and chip factories.
- NVIDIA chips are the collateral in those deals; deals are non-binding (not final).
- After the news, NVIDIA share fell over 13% at one point, then partly recovered.
- Raymond James analyst (Sunaina Sinha Haldea): companies may renegotiate terms and ask higher returns, but money is not fleeing.
- Saxo analyst (Charu Chanana): not a financing crisis, but Wall Street may be re-pricing AI risk.
- NVIDIA's Q3 results on Nov 17 are the first without Huang.
