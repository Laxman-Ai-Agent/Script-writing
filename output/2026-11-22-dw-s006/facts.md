# Google Gemini 4 Hydrogen: cheap, fast daily-driver model

## What it is
- Gemini 4 Hydrogen is a Google model built for speed and low cost, for agentic coding and everyday tasks.
- Google calls it its "daily driver"; Google's bigger Carbon and Argon models are for deep, long reasoning.
- Available via Gemini API, AI Studio, Gemini app and Google Search.

## How it works / how it happened
- Four thinking levels: low, medium, high, xhigh; more thinking means more quality, more time and more cost.
- Antigravity tool's Orchestrator feature: calls Carbon for hard thinking inside one session and gives fast tasks to Hydrogen.
- Terminal-Bench score: 18.2% for Gemini 3.8 Flash, 57% for Hydrogen (per Google).

## Why
- Developers make hundreds of small calls a day; a cheap, fast model keeps cost and waiting low.
- Script's view: AI race is won by enough intelligence plus speed and low price at high volume, not only the smartest model.

## Twist / surprising facts
- Ranked 5th on Agent Arena, so not the best model.
- All benchmark numbers come from Google and its partners; no independent expert reviews yet at time of script.
- Terminal-Bench tasks: building Linux from source, training an ML model, configuring old systems; automated tests check results.
- Default "high" setting: about 2.5x faster than 3.8 Flash (per Google).
- Partner companies reported 2 to 2.8x speed and 12 to 23% fewer tokens.
- Accepts up to 1 million tokens input at the same flat price.
