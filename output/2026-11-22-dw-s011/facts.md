# Can humans live to 150? AI, gene editing and aging

## What it is
- The 150-year figure comes from a math extrapolation, not from a person who lived that long.
- Record: Jeanne Calment, 122 years 164 days, died 1997; no one has passed it.
- Aging theory (David Sinclair, Harvard): aging comes from cells losing track of their "instructions" (bookmarks in DNA), not from damage. Still a theory, not accepted by all scientists.

## How it works / how it happened
- 2020: Sinclair team put three Yamanaka factors (genes that push cells toward a younger state) into mice eyes.
- Damaged optic nerve regrew; mice with glaucoma regained about half of lost vision; one-year-old old mice also improved. Mice only.
- OpenAI and Retro Biosciences built GPT-4b micro, which redesigned two of the Yamanaka factors.
- Insilico Medicine: AI found a new target for IPF lung disease and designed the drug.
- 71 patients, 12-week trial: drug group lung capacity rose slightly, placebo group fell.
- Life Biosciences got FDA permission in January 2026 for a trial putting cell-rejuvenation genes into human eyes.
- Casgevy (CRISPR gene editing) approved by FDA in 2023 for sickle cell disease.
- Towana Looney received a pig kidney with 10 genetic changes; it worked about 130 days.

## Why
- Aging slowdown is seen as the real route to longer lives; the script's "realistic win" is more healthy years, not just more years.
- Resilience (how fast the body recovers from injury) drops with age; extrapolated to zero at 120-150 years.

## Twist / surprising facts
- Sweden: AI-supported mammography on over 100,000 women found 29% more breast cancers.
- Retro's GPT-4b micro results claimed about 50x better in lab; not yet checked by outside experts.
- Sam Altman put $180 million into Retro; goal is 10 more healthy years for humans.
- Altos Labs started with about $3 billion.
- AlphaFold predicted about 200 million protein shapes; its creators won the 2024 Nobel prize.
- Olshansky 2024: lifespan gains slowing; chance of reaching 100 at most 15% of women and 5% of men.
- Average global life expectancy fell 1.8 years between 2019 and 2021, erasing about ten years of progress.
- Average global life expectancy now about 71.
- Resveratrol and NAD boosters worked in mice but had weak human proof.
- Unity Biotechnology knee pain drug did not beat placebo.
