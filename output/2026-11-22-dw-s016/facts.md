# Anthropic IPO roadshow: 825 million shares at $70-78 on Nasdaq ("ANT")

## What it is
- Anthropic (Claude AI maker; founded 2021 by Dario Amodei and Daniela Amodei, ex-OpenAI) started its IPO roadshow.
- It is selling 825 million Class A shares, priced $70-78 each, on Nasdaq under ticker ANT.
- Top price raises about $64 billion; banks have an option for about 124 million extra shares if demand is high.

## How it works / how it happened
- Nov 10: roadshow began (company explains business to large investors; banks collect orders and fix final price by demand).
- Nov 18 (expected): final price fixed.
- Nov 19 (expected): Nasdaq trading starts; no one can buy before that.
- Nvidia, AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft and TSMC (chip, cloud and memory suppliers) will buy about $26 billion of Class N shares at IPO price.
- Class N shares carry no voting rights; with these buys, total deal is about $90 billion.

## Why
- Chip and cloud suppliers become shareholders; script says money is coming in fast.
- Compute (AI chips, data centers) costs are rising just as fast; company says IPO money will mostly go to compute.

## Twist / surprising facts
- Valuation at this price range: about $1.4 trillion (reports); May private funding valued it at $965 billion (reports).
- Summer talk of up to $2 trillion was higher than the current range.
- Revenue of about $17 billion in July-September quarter.
- Same quarter: operating loss of about $0.5 billion; previous quarter had $974 million profit.
- Loss reasons: about $5 billion in short-term compute bought at high prices; about $2 billion non-cash charge from an employee donation match program.
- Company adjusted profit: $2.5 billion (company's own calculation).
- Compute commitments by end of September: about $895 billion.
- Filing about 300 pages, about 90 on risks (AP report); company itself wrote that safety measures are not complete.
- Anthropic is a public benefit corporation; Long-Term Benefit Trust can elect board majority, so shareholders get profit share but not full control (analysts).
- The deal could beat SpaceX's June IPO record (reports).
- First-nine-months 2026 revenue is about 7 times all of 2025 revenue (SiliconANGLE calculation).
