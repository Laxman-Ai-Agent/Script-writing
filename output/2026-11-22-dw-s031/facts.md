# GTA 6: Rockstar, Vice City, and the Bengaluru team

## What it is
- GTA 6 released on November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S; PC version not yet out.
- GTA = Grand Theft Auto, an open-world crime series by Rockstar Games (owned by Take-Two Interactive).
- Set in Leonida (Florida-like state); Vice City is a part of it; Leonida Keys and Grassrivers (Everglades-like) also included.
- Main characters: Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, a couple of criminals inspired by Bonnie and Clyde; first female lead in the series.

## How it works / how it happened
- 1997: DMA Design (Dundee, Scotland) made Race'n'Chase, a police chase game; team found it boring.
- They switched the player from police to thief; first Grand Theft Auto came out in 1997 (2D, top-down).
- Brazil banned the first game.
- 1998: Sam and Dan Houser started Rockstar Games.
- 2002: DMA Design became Rockstar North.
- 2001 GTA III moved the series to 3D; 2002 Vice City; 2004 San Andreas; 2008 GTA IV; 2013 GTA V.
- Rockstar India studio in Bengaluru, started 2016; reports say many GTA 6 developers work there (not officially confirmed).

## Why
- Budget is estimated above $1 billion (industry estimate); Rockstar has not given an official number.
- Movie comparison: a big Hollywood blockbuster costs about $150-300 million.
- Series sells well: franchise earns over $10 billion (script says 1000 crore dollars).

## Twist / surprising facts
- Open-world design: story missions or free roaming by car.
- Cities are satire of real places: Vice City = Miami, Los Santos = Los Angeles, Liberty City = New York.
- GTA V sold over 225 million copies (script: 22.5 crore); whole series over 460 million (script: 46 crore).
- First GTA 6 trailer (December 2023) got over 90 million views in 24 hours; script says a Guinness record.
- Map reported about 3x bigger than Red Dead Redemption 2.
- India PS5 price: Standard Rs 5,999; Ultimate Rs 7,499.
- Some claim Metacritic score 99 and pre-orders over $1.65 billion; not confirmed by any official source.
