- Aranya 67: Rama finds Jatayu bloodied, first suspects him of being a rakshasa who harmed Sita; Jatayu says Ravana took her; Rama drops his bow, embraces him, grieves — https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/the-ramayana-of-valmiki/d/doc424164.html — high

- Jatayu dies mid-account; Rama says he grieves this bird more than Sita's loss, treats him like Dasharatha, builds the pyre with Lakshmana, offers rohi deer flesh, recites Vedic formulas, then bathes and offers udaka (water) at the Godavari; Jatayu reaches the heavenly place of saints — same source — high

## Angle
- Jatayu owed nothing to Sita: a 60,000-year-old, tired vulture chose to fight a young armed Ravana, knowing he would lose, purely for a friendship with a dead king (Dasharatha). No reward.
- He does real damage (bows, shield, chariot, mules, charioteer, canopy) and his last act is the first clue: direction south + Ravana's name and parentage. Rama gives him the rites of a father.
- Misconception: Valmiki has no Sita message/jewel/ring handed to Jatayu and no miracle that keeps him alive till Rama arrives; Lepakshi's "Rise, bird" scene is not Valmiki.
- Misconception: the sun-flight burning is Sampati's wings (Jatayu only scorched/saved) in Valmiki.

## Caveats
- Sarga numbering differs between editions (critical vs vulgate; Shastri chapters offset); treat as medium.
- Translations differ on arm-cutting, "donkeys" vs "mules", and "vinda/vindya".
- valmikiramayan.net pages returned server errors; content comes from wisdomlib (Shastri translation), sanskritdocuments, search snippets. Verse numbers and exact Sanskrit unverified.
- Kamba Ramayana, Ramcharitmanas and Adhyatma Ramayana differences were NOT verified; do not state in script.
