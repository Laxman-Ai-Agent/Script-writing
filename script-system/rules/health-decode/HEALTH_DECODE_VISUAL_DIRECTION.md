## 1. Strict Text Separation Rule (Inside Image vs. On Top of Image)
Follow this two-stage separation rule without exception:

- **Stage 1 — Image Generation (ZERO Text Inside the AI Image):**
  - When generating `ai_image` assets, there must be **ZERO text, letters, numbers, or labels inside the generated image artwork itself**.
  - Every image prompt must explicitly include: `"pure clean 3D medical illustration, zero text inside image, no labels, no letters, no numbers, no watermark"`. The saved image file must be purely clean visual artwork.

- **Stage 2 — Video Editing / Remotion (Mandatory Text ON TOP of the Image):**
  - After clean AI images are generated, **Remotion video editing MUST place bold typography, anatomical callouts, and captions ON TOP of the image** as animated overlay layers.
  - All organ names (`Liver`, `Visceral Fat`, `Muscles`), bold highlight keywords, pointer lines, and subtitles are rendered crisply during video composition—never baked into the AI image.
